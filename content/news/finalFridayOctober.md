Friday, October 30th two OpenStudios locations will have Final Friday events!

**Lawrence Percolator:** Local artist and printmaker [Michael Taylor (M.T.)](https://pixninja.com) will have a solo show at Lawrence Percolator's location at 646 Mass St (formerly Squishington's) from 5pm-9pm.

**Lawrence Potters' Guild:** Over 12 artists will have their work on display with refreshments (and a chance of live banjo...) from 5pm-9pm at 938 Mass St (next to Red Lyon Tavern).
