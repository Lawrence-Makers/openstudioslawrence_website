Aelicia will show her 30+ year retrospective of queer, activist, punk, hip-hop, and subculture photography! There will be a DJ, live music, selfie stations, food, art vendors, jewerly and more! See the art of the people who made Lawrence LFK!

Read about it in [The Lawrence Times](https://lawrencekstimes.com/2026/09/22/punk-queer-activist-art-show-pre/)!

This is the Livingroom Collective's last Final Friday as part of OpenStudios Lawrence so you don't want to miss this! Next month 938 Mass St. will host the [Lawrence Potter's Guild](https://lawrencepottersguild.com/).
