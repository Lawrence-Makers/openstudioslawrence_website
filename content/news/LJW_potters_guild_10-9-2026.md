The Lawrence Journal-World's Town Talk wrote about the [Lawrence Potters' Guild](https://lawrencepottersguild.com/)'s new OpenStudios space at 938 Massachusetts St, next to [Red Lyon Tavern](https://www.facebook.com/RedLyonTavern/). The guild's 30 members have the storefront through at least November 8, and will be open for the Final Friday art walk on October 30. A portion of every sale supports the guild's fine arts scholarships.
